PromptX Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies specifically to PromptX, a fully offline desktop teleprompter application for Windows.

100% Local & Offline Operation

PromptX operates entirely on your local machine. Your scripts, settings, and session data remain exclusively on your device and are never uploaded, transmitted, or shared externally.

Your teleprompter scripts and creative work remain fully private and under your control at all times.

Data We Do Not Collect

PromptX does not collect or process personal information.

  • No account creation is required.
  • No names, emails, phone numbers, or passwords are requested.
  • No analytics or telemetry systems are used.
  • No advertisements are displayed.
  • No internet connectivity is required.
  • No tracking of scripts or usage behavior occurs.
  • No microphone, camera, or clipboard access is performed.
  • No cloud synchronization exists.

Local Settings Storage

PromptX stores user preferences locally on your computer, including settings such as font size, colors, opacity, alignment, and scroll speed.

%AppData%\PromptX\settings.json

This file remains entirely on your device and may be deleted at any time to restore default settings.

Imported Scripts

Scripts imported into PromptX are loaded locally for display purposes only. Imported content is not transmitted, indexed, analyzed, or permanently stored by the application.

Secure Distribution

PromptX is distributed as a signed Windows application package to help ensure secure installation and clean removal from the system.

No Internet Communication

PromptX performs no background network communication, update checks, analytics requests, advertising requests, or remote connections during normal operation.
