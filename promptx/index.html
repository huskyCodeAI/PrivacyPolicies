PromptX — Application-Specific Privacy Details

The following sections describe privacy practices specific to PromptX. PromptX is a fully offline desktop teleprompter and does not activate any of the third-party services listed above. It has no internet connectivity, no ads, and no analytics.

PromptX — 100% Local & Offline Processing

PromptX operates entirely on your local machine. Your scripts, configuration preferences, and session state are processed exclusively on your device and are never uploaded, transmitted, or shared with any external server or third party.

Your teleprompter scripts and creative work are your absolute property. PromptX has zero access to your content beyond displaying it on screen during your session.

PromptX — Data We Do Not Collect

We believe in absolute user privacy. PromptX:

  • Does not require creating or logging into a user account to function.
  • Does not request names, emails, phone numbers, or passwords.
  • Does not track your scripts, session duration, scroll history, or feature usage.
  • Does not collect telemetry, crash reports, or system diagnostics.
  • Does not access your microphone, camera, clipboard, or screen beyond what you explicitly load into the app.
  • Does not display advertisements of any kind.
  • Makes zero network requests during normal operation.

PromptX — Local Settings Storage

PromptX saves your preferences (scroll speed, font size, text and background colors, overlay opacity, alignment, and scroll mode) to a local JSON file at:

%AppData%\PromptX\settings.json

This file remains entirely on your computer and is never transmitted externally. You can delete it at any time to restore factory defaults.

PromptX — Script Import

PromptX allows you to import plain-text scripts from your local file system. The imported content is loaded into memory for display only — it is not indexed, stored beyond your session, or transmitted to any external service.

PromptX — Secure MSIX Distribution

PromptX is distributed through the Microsoft Store as a signed MSIX package. This packaging sandboxes the application, preventing unauthorized system-level modifications and ensuring a completely clean uninstallation with zero registry residue.

PromptX — No Internet Connectivity

PromptX requires no internet connection to function. There are no update checks, license verification calls, analytics pings, or advertising network requests built into the application.

Children's Privacy

PromptX does not collect data from any user, including children. The application is safe for use in all educational, broadcast, and professional environments without privacy concerns.
