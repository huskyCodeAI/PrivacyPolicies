Neon Clock Widget Pro Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies specifically to Neon Clock Widget Pro, a fully offline customizable neon glow clock widget application for Windows.

100% Local & Offline Operation

Neon Clock Widget Pro operates entirely on your local machine. All layout rendering, time synchronizations with your local system clock, and configuration saving happen locally on your device and are never uploaded, transmitted, or shared externally.

Your system data and preferences remain fully private and under your control at all times. Neon Clock Widget Pro never reads, copies, or transmits your system data outside your computer.

Data We Do Not Collect

Neon Clock Widget Pro does not collect or process personal information.

  • No account creation is required.
  • No names, emails, phone numbers, or passwords are requested.
  • No analytics, tracking, or telemetry systems are used.
  • No advertisements are displayed.
  • No active internet connection is required or used during normal clock operation.
  • No tracking of user preferences or behavior occurs.
  • No cloud synchronization features exist.

Local Settings Storage

Neon Clock Widget Pro stores user configuration preferences locally on your computer, including the selected font styles, neon day/date colors, active clock style, and window coordinates.

%LocalAppData%\Neon_Clock_Widget_Pro\...

These preferences remain entirely on your device and may be cleared by resetting the app's settings. No data is persisted on external servers.

No External Network Communication

Neon Clock Widget Pro performs no background network communication, update checks, analytics requests, or remote server connections.
