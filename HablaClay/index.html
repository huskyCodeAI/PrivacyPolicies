Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 22, 2026

Husky Estudio Soft ("we", "our", or "us") operates the HablaClay application. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This privacy policy explains our practices regarding data collection and usage within the application.

1. Information Collection and Use

HablaClay does not directly collect, store, share, or monetize any personal data, voice recordings, or biometric information. No account sign-up, email registration, or social profile integration is required to use this application.

2. Voice Input Processing (Microphone)

To enable the Spanish pronunciation evaluation feature, HablaClay requests system permission to access your microphone. Please note the following regarding your audio data:

  • The voice-to-text conversion is processed through the host operating system's native speech recognition interfaces (via the Chromium Web Speech API in WebView2).
  • Husky Estudio Soft does not transmit, receive, or store your voice recording data on any servers owned or managed by us.
  • All speech recognition operations are subject to the native privacy terms of the host platform provider (Microsoft/Windows).

3. Local Progress Storage

All application progress, lesson scores, study statistics, and completed achievements are saved strictly locally on your physical device. We do not host cloud databases or run online trackers to capture your progress or learning behavior.

4. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
