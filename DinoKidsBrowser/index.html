Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 11, 2026

At DinoBrowser Premium ("we", "our"), the safety and privacy of children and families is our top priority. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information Collection

DinoBrowser Premium is a local web browser focused on parental control. We do not collect or sell personal data to third parties. All parental control settings, website blocks, and preferences are stored encrypted and locally on your device.

  • Local Data: Usage history, security PINs, and white/black lists are stored exclusively on your computer using military-grade encryption (SQLCipher and DPAPI).
  • Network Requests: The browser uses Cloudflare DNS for Families to filter adult content and malware, according to their standard DNS resolution policies.

2. Features for Children

Our software includes mini-games and educational features (Dino Adventure) that are completely offline. They do not include ad trackers, hidden in-app purchases, or invasive telemetry. We strictly comply with child privacy laws (like COPPA).

3. Purchases and Subscriptions

DinoBrowser Premium manages purchases and subscriptions through the secure infrastructure of the Microsoft Store. We do not store or process credit card or payment information on our servers.

4. System Permissions

To provide a safe browsing environment and block access to inappropriate sites, DinoBrowser may require elevated system permissions (Kiosk Mode and local Hosts file manipulation). These permissions are only used for content filtering and security.

5. Contact

If you have any questions about our privacy practices, please contact our technical support team through the Microsoft Store portal or the parent dashboard within the app.
