PhotoAura Studio

Simplified Privacy Policy

1. 100% Local & Private Processing

PhotoAura Studio is an offline creative suite. Your images, canvases, graphic projects, and files are processed strictly locally on your computer. None of your media or creative content is uploaded, transmitted, or stored on external servers.

Your creations are your absolute property. PhotoAura Studio has zero access to your files unless you explicitly choose to open or save them on your local hard drive.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

We believe in absolute user privacy. Unlike cloud-based graphic tools, PhotoAura Studio:

  • Does not require creating or logging into a user account to function.
  • Does not request names, emails, phone numbers, or passwords.
  • Does not track your design habits, history, or tool usage.
  • Does not collect telemetry, system metrics, or crash logs.

3. Safe & Sandboxed Execution

Our application is distributed as a secure, signed MSIX container via the Windows Store. This packaging sandboxes the application, preventing it from making unauthorized system-wide modifications and ensuring a completely clean uninstallation with zero registry residue.

4. Open Source & Licensing

PhotoAura Studio is built upon the powerful open-source GIMP (GNU Image Manipulation Program) engine and is distributed in full compliance with the GNU General Public License (GPLv3). The custom C# native launcher designed for PhotoAura Studio does not make network connections and runs purely offline to configure your dark purple visual theme and workspace preferences.
