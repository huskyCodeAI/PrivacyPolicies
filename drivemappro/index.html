DriveMap Pro Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies specifically to DriveMap Pro, a fully offline disk space analyzer application for Windows.

100% Local & Offline Operation

DriveMap Pro operates entirely on your local machine. All disk scanning, file analysis, and data processing happen locally on your device and are never uploaded, transmitted, or shared externally.

Your file system data remains fully private and under your control at all times. DriveMap Pro never copies, sends, or stores your file information outside your computer.

Data We Do Not Collect

DriveMap Pro does not collect or process personal information.

  • No account creation is required.
  • No names, emails, phone numbers, or passwords are requested.
  • No analytics or telemetry systems are used.
  • No advertisements are displayed.
  • No internet connectivity is required.
  • No tracking of scanned files or usage behavior occurs.
  • No cloud synchronization exists.
  • No file contents are read or transmitted — only file names and sizes are analyzed.

Local Settings Storage

DriveMap Pro stores user preferences locally on your computer, including the selected theme (dark/light), window size, and last scanned path.

%AppData%\DriveMapPro\user.config

This file remains entirely on your device and may be deleted at any time to restore default settings. No scan results or file metadata are persisted between sessions.

No Internet Communication

DriveMap Pro performs no background network communication, update checks, analytics requests, advertising requests, or remote connections during normal operation.
